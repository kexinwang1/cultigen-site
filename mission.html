When the labor is handled, what should we be learning?

Large language models and physical AI are taking over more of our routine mental and manual work. They hand back something scarce: time, and room to create.

We think what we should be learning is the frontier. In an age where anyone can be a creator, the rarest thing is not another finished tool, but the ability to understand the newest research while it is still new. AI can explain and build almost anything, yet it is the people who truly understand the frontier who decide where it goes. Understanding is the line between using what already exists and creating what does not.

For now, the frontier is a concentrated asset, locked inside a few elite labs and institutions, within reach of a small number of people. Cultigen turns the world's most advanced technology into courses you can actually build, learned anywhere and, wherever we can, together and in person. The edge of what is known should not be a privilege. It should be a shared, global asset.

When the frontier belongs to everyone, the next wave of invention gets far larger. AI freed our creativity. We want to use it to free much more of it, and let human progress compound.

Kexin Wang · Founder
