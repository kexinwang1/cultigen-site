Why I'm building
Cultigen.

A note from the founder, and why the frontier belongs in everyone's hands.

In about a year, I taught myself from fashion design into engineering, from a first research job at Tsinghua to Brown, RISD, and MIT. None of it came from a normal path. It came from getting my hands on the real thing, early.

I'm a researcher at MIT CSAIL, in human-computer interaction and digital fabrication, the exact craft behind Cultigen's interactives and kits. I've watched brilliant research stay locked in papers, and curious people stay locked out of it.

Cultigen is how I put the frontier into everyone's hands.

Kexin WangFounder
"A degree measures opportunity, not ability. The frontier should be something you can reach and build, not something you need the right credential to touch."

Course quality is a bigger
problem than it looks.

When teaching drifts, the cost lands on real people: students, graduates, and the public that funds research.

Is a degree still worth it?

In the AI age, more and more people question the value of higher education. The answer isn't a smaller university. It's making what happens inside one undeniably good.

Big debt, uneven teaching

Students take on serious debt for degrees whose teaching quality still has enormous room to improve. If the price is real, the learning should be too.

Taught vs. actually needed

What schools teach and what the world needs have drifted apart. Graduates leave underprepared and talent goes to waste, while employers still can't fill the roles they have. A course in step with the frontier closes both gaps at once.

Research stuck at the frontier

The path from a new discovery to a teachable lesson is slow and costly, so learners keep meeting yesterday's material. Better tools shorten that path.

Knowledge that never arrives

Too much research stops at "published." Work that could reach and benefit people never becomes something you can learn from and build on.

None of this means schools are obsolete.

It means the quality of the course is the thing worth fixing, and the thing Cultigen is built to fix.

Questions, answered.

What is Cultigen?

Cultigen is a course engine that turns frontier research, an industry process, or a product into a complete hands-on course: a lesson, a working interactive, and a buildable kit.

What can Cultigen turn into a course?

A frontier research paper, an industry process, or a real product. Cultigen turns any of them into a lesson, a working interactive, and a buildable hands-on kit.

What makes a Cultigen course accurate?

Every course is reviewed by experts in the field, so being up to date never means being unvetted.

What is FunguyLab?

FunguyLab is Cultigen's first course brand: a hands-on kit that uses light and AI to grow living fungi into designed forms, built from research published at SIGGRAPH.

Why hands-on?

Ideas stick when you build them. A lesson explains the concept, an interactive lets you play with it, and a kit lets you make it with your own hands.
