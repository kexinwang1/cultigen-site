Course brands

Cultigen doesn't stop at a lesson.

It launches each course as a course brand: something with a name, an identity, and a community that people are proud to be part of and want to share.

FunguyLab is the first. It uses light and AI to grow living fungi into designed forms, a hands-on kit built from our own research published at SIGGRAPH. It launched a month ago and reached the front page of r/mycology in a day.
