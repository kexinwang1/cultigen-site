FunguyLab:
our first course brand.

FunguyLab is the first course to come out of Cultigen. It uses light and AI to grow living fungi into designed forms, a hands-on kit that teaches biology, engineering, and design at once, built from my own research published at SIGGRAPH.

It launched a month ago and took off on its own: the front page of r/mycology in a day, covered by Hackaday, and heading to exhibitions this fall. It is the first of many.
