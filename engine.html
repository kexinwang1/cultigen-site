Cultigen is a course engine.

Give it the newest technology, a research paper, an industry process, or a product, and it returns a complete hands-on course: a lesson that gets you to the real idea, a working interactive you can play with, and a kit you build with your own hands.

Every course is reviewed by the original researchers, so it stays accurate and current. What used to take years now takes minutes.

You can try it yourself on the home page: drop in a link or a PDF and watch it sketch a course.
